02 — About

The short version

I sit at the join between technology, operations and the business — the place where strategy either becomes real or quietly stalls.

Over ten years at Tillo I've held the CTO, COO and CIO seats — often more than one at a time — through the messy, rewarding work of scaling a B2B SaaS business. That range is deliberate: the hardest problems in a growing company rarely respect the org chart, and the leaders who move the needle are the ones fluent in engineering, operations and commercial reality at once.

I care about building teams that ship, systems that scale, and decisions that hold up under pressure. Increasingly I'm thinking and writing about how AI reshapes leadership itself — not the tooling, but the judgement, accountability and pace it demands of the people at the top.

Away from the desk you'll find me on the water as Vice-Commodore of Sailing at CMYC, or comparing notes with fellow leaders at Brighton CTO.
